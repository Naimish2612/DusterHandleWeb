DEFINING ARCHITECTURAL PRECISION.

Duster was founded on a singular obsession: to elevate functional hardware into objects of structural permanence and tactile luxury. We do not manufacture parts; we engineer the critical touchpoints of the built environment.

CORE DOCTRINES

01 / FOUNDATION

PRECISION ENGINEERING

Tolerances measured in microns. Every piece is CNC milled from solid billets, ensuring structural integrity that far exceeds industry standards. A ruthless commitment to exactitude.

SUSTAINABLE SOURCING

Utilizing 100% recycled architectural-grade alloys where possible. Our supply chain is transparent, rigorous, and designed to minimize environmental impact without compromising material strength.

BESPOKE CRAFTSMANSHIP

The synthesis of advanced robotics and human finishing. After machining, every surface is hand-polished or brushed to achieve a tactile response that is distinctively Duster.

LINEAGE

02 / HISTORY
2005

Inception

Founded as a bespoke prototyping studio for high-end residential architecture in Milan. The initial focus was purely on resolving complex structural joint issues with elegant, minimalist solutions.

2012

The Monolith Collection

Launch of our first commercial line, defining our signature aesthetic: heavy, unornamented geometries milled from solid brass and stainless steel. Recognized globally for introducing tactile weight back into hardware.

2024

Global Infrastructure

Expansion of precision manufacturing facilities. Today, Duster hardware is specified in landmark architectural projects across five continents, remaining true to our founding principle of uncompromising exactitude.
